The Premise

Uniting Two
Creative Worlds.

The Forge™ Editor's Jam is not a standard game jam. It is a 48-hour pressure cooker designed to unite Video Editors and Web Developers.

Their goal? To push the world's most advanced browser-based non-linear video editor to its absolute limits by cutting a cinematic short, or coding the extensions to make it happen.

The Editors

Tasked with crafting a 90-second visual masterpiece from raw b-roll, focusing purely on narrative and pacing.

The Developers

Tasked with writing vanilla .js extensions, custom transitions, and QoL workflow tools live during the event.

The Engine

WASM Video Editor

100% Client-Side

Powered by WebAssembly. No servers, no accounts, no paywalls. The user's browser is the entire render farm.

IndexedDB Auto-Save

Continuous native saves. If a tab closes or the browser crashes, zero timeline progress is ever lost.

Safe Mode Engineering

Built for hackers. Append ?safemode=true to bypass broken custom extensions without crashing the UI.

Logistics

Bulletproof Infrastructure

We anticipate chaos. Our Discord and technical pipelines are designed to handle it effortlessly.

Discord Gatekeeping

  • #introductions verification: New joins cannot see the server until they post and receive the @Jammer role via Bot. Neutralizes spam bots instantly.
  • Team Voice Rooms: Voice channels are hard-capped at 5 users to prevent massive, noisy lobbies and foster team intimacy.

itch.io "Opt-Out" Sorting

  • One Submission Page: All entries go to one single page. Solo Vanilla Editors simply check an "Opt-out of Technical Innovation" box.
  • Automated Leaderboards: This natively generates two perfectly clean, separate leaderboards for Editors and Developers without manual tracking.

The VIP Judge.

We know you are incredibly busy. Our judging pipeline is designed to require absolutely zero effort from you during the 48-hour event itself.

Phase 1: Community Vote (1 Week)

Only Jammers can vote. The community tests the extensions and watches the videos throughout the week, organically filtering out the noise.

Phase 2: Your Role (The Top 30%)

You only evaluate a highly curated shortlist of the absolute best cuts. We jump into a 1-hour Discord Watch Party, review the Top 30% together, and finalize the placements.
